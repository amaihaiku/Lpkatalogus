## 2025-05-27 - Double Indirection Bottleneck in Z80 Memory Access
**Learning:** In the Z80 emulator loop (`Z80Run` and `Z80Interrupt`), the `Z80ReadMem` and `Z80WriteMem` macros are executed millions of times per second. The original implementation used double indirection via `mappedMemory[bank]->data[address]` and `mappedMemory[bank]->isDirty`. The `mappedMemory` array holds pointers to `MemoryPage` objects. This extra indirection is extremely costly in the tight inner loop of the CPU emulator, acting as a major bottleneck.
**Action:** Always inspect the tight inner loops of emulation or render cycles for double indirection (pointer-to-pointer-to-data). Flattening these by maintaining a direct array of pointers to the inner data (e.g., `uint8_t* mappedMemoryData[4]`) updated during page switching is highly effective at reducing CPU cycles while keeping readability intact.
